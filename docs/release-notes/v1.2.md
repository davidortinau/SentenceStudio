## Native Apps, Sam and Known Words

### New Apps

- **Native iPhone and iPad App** — SentenceStudio rebuilt in SwiftUI, with Study, Library and Settings tabs, system lists, forms and search
- **Native Android App** — The same journeys in Jetpack Compose with Material 3
- **iPad and Unfolded Phone Layouts** — The Library and Settings show a sidebar beside the open page, like the system apps

### Meet Sam

- **Sam, Your Learning Coach** — Ask Sam about your words, plan and progress from any signed-in screen
- **Sam Can Do It for You** — Sam can find, add, edit and delete words and example sentences, with your confirmation first
- **What Sam Remembers** — Review, edit or forget what Sam remembers about you in Settings
- **Send Feedback** — Report a problem or an idea through Sam

### New Activities

- **Conversation Voice Mode** — Talk out loud with your conversation partner in real time
- **Number Drill** — Practice Korean numbers, counters, times and dates, with "I was correct" when the grader is too strict
- **Minimal Pairs** — Train your ear on sounds that are easy to confuse
- **Diary, How Do You Say and Word Association** — Three new ways to practice producing language
- **Recent Lookups** — The words you looked up while reading and writing, ready to save

### Vocabulary

- **You Decide What Is Known** — Mark words Known, Learning or New yourself, one at a time or many at once; choose Learner-led or Practice-led in Settings
- **Duplicate Clean-up** — Merge duplicate words in one step; Sam resolves the unclear ones by meaning, and every decision can be undone
- **Example Sentences** — Words carry reference sentences, from AI or your own
- **Idioms and Phrases** — Words, phrases and idioms are told apart and tracked separately
- **Share to Vocabulary** — Send text from other iPhone apps straight into your word list

### Import

- **Import Content** — Paste text, transcripts or word lists; the format is detected and duplicates are shown before anything is saved
- **YouTube on Every Client** — Import single videos and follow channels from the native apps too

### Sync and Your Data

- **Every Client in Sync** — Quiz history, Number Drill, Minimal Pairs, lookups, scenes and example sentences sync between the web and your devices
- **Your Data Stays Yours** — Every word, sentence and record belongs to one learner, and devices sync only their own learner's data
- **Safer Updates** — The app backs up its database before upgrading it

### Improvements

- **Korean Everywhere** — The whole app is available in Korean
- **Activity Log** — A practice calendar of what you studied and when
- **Vocabulary Quiz** — Sentence hints at your level, photo prompts and resuming exactly where you left off
